Field Log Support

For help with the Lower Esopus Field Log app, email jguzman12@manhattan.edu.

Include your phone model, app version (at the bottom of Connection), and the status or error you saw. Do not include your upload key or private field notes.

Pending uploads

Pending readings are stored on your device. When online, open the app and tap Sync now. Check Last successful upload in Connection. The phone decides when background sync runs. Contact your team's workbook administrator if you need a connection URL or upload key.

Before replacing your phone

In Records, tap Export CSV to back up observations, especially any that are still pending. Uninstalling the app removes the device's local records.
